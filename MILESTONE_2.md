# Milestone 2 — Art, animation, arena and audio pass

This update adds:
- More detailed layered fighter silhouettes: torso/shoulder definition, facial features, trunks piping, boots, gloves and independently pivoted arms.
- Lightweight procedural idle motion and hit reactions.
- Impact flash effect on successful punches.
- Original synthesized arcade sound effects: light punch, heavy punch, block and round bell.
- Generated material swatches for canvas, leather and trim.
- Existing fixed up-down camera, 8 opponents, touch controls and championship ladder retained.

## Production caveat
These are meaningful prototype upgrades, not final AAA/PS2-era production assets. The fighters are still assembled from procedural meshes rather than sculpted, UV-mapped, skinned character models. Animation is procedural rather than motion-captured. The audio is synthesized prototype SFX, not a mastered soundtrack or recorded crowd. Android APK export and device QA still require Godot export templates and the Android SDK.

The project is not padded to 100 MB. Reaching that size legitimately requires a larger authored asset library: rigged character models, animation clips, arena textures, crowd loops, music, UI art and localized voice work.
